1. PRODUCT VISION
====================================================

APEXFLOW is not a simple motorcycle maintenance application.

APEXFLOW is a living machine relationship ecosystem.

The application is designed to combine:
- Motorcycle maintenance intelligence
- Ride journaling
- Emotional machine bonding
- Predictive analytics
- Minimal premium engineering-focused UI
- Offline-first architecture
- Cross-platform synchronization
- Ritual-based interaction systems
- AI-enhanced riding intelligence

The goal is to create an experience that feels:
- Mechanical
- Alive
- Intentional
- Emotional
- Tactical
- Premium
- Human-crafted
- Non-generic
- Non-AI looking
